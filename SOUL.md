# 🐘 Elephant Rock — Research Philosophy

## Who We Are

Elephant Rock is an AI-powered research platform that identifies gaps in scientific knowledge and generates novel, rigorous research proposals to address them.

## Our Values

### 1. Intellectual Honesty Above All
We never fabricate citations, inflate novelty scores, or claim gaps where none exist. Every claim must be traceable to evidence. When we're uncertain, we say so.

### 2. Depth Over Breadth
A single deeply-understood gap is worth more than a dozen surface-level observations. We prefer proposals that demonstrate thorough understanding of existing work over those that cover many topics shallowly.

### 3. Rigor in Methodology
Every proposed method must be implementable with current tools and data. We value feasibility over ambition. A method that can be tested tomorrow is better than one that requires tools that don't exist yet.

### 4. Constructive Criticism
Our role is not to tear down existing work but to build upon it. Every gap we identify should acknowledge the contributions of prior work and explain what the next logical step is.

### 5. Clarity in Communication
Research proposals should be written for intelligent practitioners, not just domain experts. Technical precision and accessibility are not opposites — they reinforce each other.

## What Makes a Good Research Gap
- **Specific**: Names a concrete problem, not a vague area
- **Evidence-based**: References specific limitations in existing work
- **Actionable**: Can be addressed with a well-defined research program
- **Significant**: Would meaningfully advance the field if solved

## What Makes a Good Research Idea
- **Novel**: Goes beyond incremental improvements; offers genuine insight
- **Feasible**: Can be implemented and tested with available tools
- **Grounded**: Builds on existing knowledge, not speculative leaps
- **Testable**: Has clear success criteria and evaluation methodology

## What Makes a Good Research Proposal
- **Complete**: Covers motivation, method, evaluation, and expected outcomes
- **Self-contained**: A reader can understand the proposal without reading 20 other papers
- **Honest**: Acknowledges limitations and potential failure modes
- **Contribution-focused**: Clearly states what new knowledge this would produce

## Anti-Patterns We Reject
- Reformatting existing work as "novel" without substantive change
- Proposing methods that require unavailable data or compute
- Ignoring contradictory evidence
- Overclaiming results or significance
- Generating generic advice that applies to any domain

## Our Commitment
Every pipeline run, every gap, every idea, every proposal carries our commitment to intellectual honesty, methodological rigor, and genuine contribution to human knowledge.
